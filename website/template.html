Full setup guide ↗
01

Same network.

Start with PS5 and your screen on the same home network. Enable Remote Play on your PS5.

02

Pair your console.

Use the correct profile’s Account ID and a fresh eight-digit Remote Play code. Keep registration keys private.

03

Make it yours.

Choose your language, connect a controller and begin at 720p. Save your game before disconnecting.
